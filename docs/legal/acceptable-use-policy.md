This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines prohibited uses of the CivicSignals platform and services operated by CivicSignals, Inc. ("CivicSignals," "we," "us," or "our"). This AUP is incorporated into the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md).

By using the Service, you agree to comply with this AUP. If you violate this policy, we may suspend or terminate your access.

---

## 1. Core Principles

CivicSignals is built to help companies selling legitimate products and services to the public sector work more efficiently by surfacing publicly available procurement and governance information. The platform is not a surveillance tool, a harassment enabler, or a mechanism for bulk outreach.

The public servants whose information appears in CivicSignals do not consent to being contacted in bulk, to having their public roles used for non-procurement purposes, or to having their information harvested and resold. We take this responsibility seriously.

---

## 2. Prohibited Uses

The following uses of the Service are prohibited, regardless of whether they violate applicable law:

### 2.1 Harassment and Stalking

- You may **not** use the Service to locate, monitor, contact, threaten, harass, or stalk any individual, including public servants, elected officials, or private persons.
- You may **not** use the Service to compile dossiers on individuals for purposes unrelated to legitimate procurement-related outreach.

### 2.2 Bulk Outreach and Spam

- You may **not** use contact records or entity data obtained through the Service to conduct bulk, automated, or unsolicited outreach campaigns.
- You may **not** export contact records and upload them to email sequencing tools (Outreach, Salesloft, Apollo, etc.) for mass cold-email campaigns unrelated to a genuine procurement signal.
- You may **not** use the Service as a lead-generation database for purposes unrelated to public-sector procurement.

**Note:** We understand that contacting a procurement officer about an open RFP using a record you found in CivicSignals is a core use case. The prohibition is on bulk, undifferentiated outreach, not on relevant, personalized outreach that a sales professional would make anyway.

### 2.3 Resale and Redistribution

- You may **not** resell, sublicense, or redistribute signal data, contact records, or entity data obtained through the Service to third parties without our written consent.
- You may **not** create a derivative data product, intelligence service, or competitive offering using data extracted from CivicSignals.
- You may **not** integrate signal data from CivicSignals into an AI training dataset or use it to train a machine learning model.

### 2.4 Scraping and Automated Access

- You may **not** scrape, crawl, spider, or otherwise systematically extract data from CivicSignals's own web interface or API beyond the limits of your plan and the API's documented rate limits.
- You may **not** use bots, automated scripts, or mechanical means to access the web interface in ways not authorized by these Terms.
- Authorized API access under your plan's rate limits is permitted and encouraged.

### 2.5 Protected Health Information and Student Data

- You may **not** use the Service to collect, store, or process Protected Health Information (PHI) as defined under HIPAA.
- You may **not** use the Service to collect, store, or process student education records covered by FERPA.
- CivicSignals does not accept BAAs (Business Associate Agreements) and is not a HIPAA Business Associate.

### 2.6 Illegal and Harmful Activities

- You may **not** use the Service for any purpose that violates applicable law, including but not limited to: violating computer fraud laws, electronic surveillance laws, data protection law, or export control regulations.
- You may **not** use the Service to facilitate discrimination based on protected characteristics.
- You may **not** use the Service to infringe intellectual property rights.

### 2.7 Platform Integrity

- You may **not** attempt to bypass or undermine workspace isolation, authentication controls, rate limits, or any other security measures.
- You may **not** submit false registration information or impersonate another person or organization.
- You may **not** interfere with the platform's availability (e.g., by generating artificial traffic, launching denial-of-service attacks, or abusing API endpoints).
- You may **not** probe, scan, or penetration-test CivicSignals infrastructure without prior written authorization from security@civicsignals.io.

## 3. Permitted Uses

The following uses are explicitly permitted and represent the intended use of the Service:

- Monitoring public procurement activity (RFPs, bids, budget decisions) relevant to your market and ICP.
- Identifying procurement officers and agency contacts from official .gov/.edu websites for targeted outreach related to open procurement opportunities.
- Tracking board meeting agendas, leadership changes, grant awards, and other public governance events as buying signals.
- Storing FOIA request records and tracking responses for investigative or procurement purposes.
- Integrating CivicSignals signals with your CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot) to update records and track pipeline.
- Using the API and SDKs to build internal tools that surface CivicSignals intelligence to your sales team.
- Journalistic and civic-tech research using the Service or the open-source Core for public-interest investigation of government procurement practices.

We will use discretion in enforcing this policy and will generally provide a warning and cure period for unintentional or minor violations.
