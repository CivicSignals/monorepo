# Welcome to CivicSignals

CivicSignals is an open-source public-sector sales intelligence platform. It monitors thousands of public-sector data sources — procurement portals, council meeting agendas, contract databases, budget documents, leadership announcements, and more — and surfaces the signals that matter to your sales team.
