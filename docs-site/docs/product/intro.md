# CivicSignals product overview

CivicSignals monitors thousands of public-sector data sources and surfaces the signals that matter to your sales team: procurement postings, contract renewals, leadership changes, budget approvals, and more.

## What CivicSignals does

Public-sector procurement moves slowly and publicly. Every RFP, every leadership hire, every budget line item is published somewhere — but the data is scattered across thousands of portals, PDFs, and government websites. CivicSignals ingests all of it and turns it into a prioritised signal feed matched to your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).

**A typical morning with CivicSignals takes 30 minutes:**

1. Open the feed — your overnight signals are already scored and ranked.
2. Work through the top 5–15 signals: read, push to your CRM, add to your pipeline, dismiss what is not relevant.
3. Use Smart Search for ad-hoc research (for example, "California community colleges considering AI procurement expiring 2026").
4. Close your laptop.

## Core concepts

| Concept | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Signal** | A discrete procurement event — an RFP posted, a contract expiring, a leadership change. Twelve signal types in MVP. |
| **Entity** | A public-sector organisation: a school district, city, county, university, or special district. Each entity has contacts, signals, and a history. |
| **ICP** | Your Ideal Customer Profile: the combination of entity types, geographies, sizes, signal types, keywords, and score threshold that determines which signals appear in your feed. |
| **Saved search** | A named filter set that runs continuously and can deliver email digests or Slack notifications. |
| **Pipeline** | A lightweight Kanban board (Saved → Researching → Contacted → Meeting Booked → Qualified → Proposal/RFP → Won/Lost) for tracking opportunities. |
| **FOIA request** | A public-records request you can draft, track, and follow up on — with pre-written templates for eight US jurisdictions. |
