Request for Proposals 2026-014

District-wide ERP Modernization

The Lone Star Independent School District (Texas) is seeking qualified vendors to design, implement, and support a district-wide Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system covering finance, human resources, payroll, and procurement for its 52,000 students and 6,800 staff.

  • RFP Number: 2026-014
  • Issuing Agency: Lone Star Independent School District
  • Estimated Contract Value: $4,800,000
  • Proposal Submission Deadline: June 30, 2026 at 2:00 PM CT
  • Primary Contact: Dana Reyes, Director of Procurement
  • Submission Portal: https://procurement.txed.example.gov/rfp/erp-2026/submit

Proposals must address data migration, single sign-on integration, and a multi-year support plan. Late submissions will not be accepted.
