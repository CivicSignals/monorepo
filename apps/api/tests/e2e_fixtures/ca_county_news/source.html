Sierra County weighs IT modernization push

By the Sierra Valley Dispatch — May 2026

Sierra County (California) officials signaled this week that a broad overhaul of the county's aging technology systems is on the horizon, with the board of supervisors directing staff to study options for replacing legacy permitting, finance, and records platforms.

"We have systems that predate the smartphone," said one supervisor during the public session. No formal solicitation has been issued yet, but the county's CIO confirmed that a request for proposals could follow later this year if the study recommends a full replacement.

The discussion was first reported by the Sierra Valley Dispatch.
